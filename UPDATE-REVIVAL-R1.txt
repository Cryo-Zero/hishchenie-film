Baseline
--------
Built on the restored pre-mockup P19 site. This patch intentionally does not
redesign the rest of the site and does not include advertising or full-film
hosting.

Frontend changes
----------------
1. Profile avatar subject-09 is excluded from every generated/rerolled avatar.
   The physical file may remain in the repository, but the client never selects it.
2. Reviews are open again and use the new write RPC submit_my_review_v5.
3. First review requires a short server-side human verification challenge.
4. Duplicate/repeated actions are guarded in the client and rate-limited server-side.
5. Feed/stats auto-sync across tabs and periodically while the page is visible.
6. Strong-language filter is optional and display-only. Database text is never altered.
7. Likes and replies remain available and use their rate-limited v2 write RPCs.
8. FAQ works like SUBJECT DOSSIER: question list stays visible, same question or X closes response to standby.
9. Trailer starts at 35% volume unless the visitor has already chosen a volume.
10. ARCHIVE is attached to the physical left browser wall. Drawer and button move together.
11. ARCHIVE selection does not close the drawer.
12. Gallery arrows are single technical chevrons with large hit areas.
13. Explicit FULLSCREEN button is removed. Clicking the image opens the custom overlay viewer.
14. Overlay viewer keeps previous/next navigation, counter and thin pagination.
15. AUTHOR FILES // VERIFIED and thin archive pagination are preserved.
16. Admin panel gets search, visibility and sort controls and uses admin_get_reviews_v2.
